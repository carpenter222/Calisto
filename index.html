Our Story

Where Italy Meets Egypt

Tucked inside Family Land Mall next to Galaxy Cinema, Calisto has grown into one of Maadi's go-to gathering spots — the kind of place people return to with family on a Friday, with friends after work, or alone with a laptop and a good coffee.

The kitchen moves comfortably between two culinary traditions: Italian classics like pizza, pasta and lasagna sit alongside Egyptian favourites such as shakshuka, molokhia and oriental grills, all served in a warm, softly-lit dining room with a distinctly relaxed, upscale feel.
